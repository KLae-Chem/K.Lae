About Me

Teaching Chemistry,
Inspiring Curiosity

I am an Organic Chemistry Teacher passionate about helping students understand the principles, reactions, and applications of organic chemistry. My teaching approach focuses on connecting fundamental concepts with practical examples, laboratory experiences, and real-world applications.

I aim to create an engaging and supportive learning environment where students can develop strong chemical knowledge, analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and confidence in applying chemistry to scientific challenges.

Beyond classroom teaching, I am interested in curriculum development, innovative chemistry education, and the integration of digital technologies into learning.
